### 🏗️ Featured Architecture: SAMA Open Banking Ecosystem
My two primary repositories operate as an integrated, multi-container Open Banking ecosystem over Docker:

* **[Fath | فتح — Saudi Open Banking Sandbox (ASPSP/TSP)](https://github.com/yamen-alwaily/fath):** Simulates SAMA AIS & PIS standards across 3 Saudi banks with pure-Python **OAuth 2.0 + PKCE (`RFC 7636`)**, **RFC 7662** token introspection, **ISO 13616 MOD-97** Saudi IBAN validation, and real-time **SocketIO** payment state streaming.
* **[Smart Wealth | الذكاء المالي — AI Fintech & AISP Client](https://github.com/yamen-alwaily/smart-wealth):** Connects to `Fath` via OAuth 2.0 PKCE to automate idempotent transaction syncing into a dual-engine **PostgreSQL 16 / SQLite** database layer, featuring a 4-pillar Shariah credit scoring engine and localized **Z-score** anomaly detection.
